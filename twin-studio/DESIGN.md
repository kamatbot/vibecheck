# Twin Studio design direction

Desktop only: a playful cosmic character instrument with a 1024px minimum canvas. The large, full-body character is the initial focal point, with identity and selfie controls on the left and compact editing tools on the right. Face and Hair tools focus the face; independent view buttons expose the current framing.

Midnight ink surfaces, orbital stage framing, mint, lilac and coral signals, geometric bold sans lettering, an angled mint title accent and a small starburst create a whimsical science-fiction mood. Keep controls readable and literal: “Start with a selfie,” Face view, Body view, Save and Export. No corporate dashboard cards or editorial serif typography.

Photos stay on device; suggestions are editable starting points, not exact reconstructions. Manual tools, keyboard focus, persistent result notices, loading/error/retry states, bounded undo/redo, and preview-dependent export availability remain visible and functional. Preserve the mounted scene node across editor renders.

The avatar itself should feel warm and creative: rounded cheek/chin proportions, bright expressive eyes with generous irises, a gentle smile, simplified flowing hair masses and playful clothing colors. Use soft portrait lighting without harsh facial self-shadows. Photo suggestions stay conservative so a person can recognize a little of themselves without losing the character's appeal.
